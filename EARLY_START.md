# Early milestone start

On Payments, either participant can choose **Start milestone early** after the
preceding milestones have completed and their payments are verified. The action
previews the exact date changes and asks both participants to approve that same
schedule using their account names. No manual amendment is required.

After the second approval, the selected milestone starts today. Its delivery
date and all later unfunded milestone dates move earlier by the same number of
calendar days, preserving the agreed work durations, review periods and revision
reserve. The final project deadline and future client dependency dates move too.
The current Agreement view, summary, funding checks and default PDF/JSON exports
use those approved dates automatically.

This creates a recorded schedule addendum with both approvals. Original signed
versions and their signatures remain available unchanged. Previously funded or
completed milestones, prices, scope and fixed holiday dates remain unchanged.
Non-working delivery dates are explicitly listed for both participants to agree.

Existing funding attempts on affected milestones, unsettled preceding payments,
open claims, another pending proposal, or a duration exceeding the funding window
block early start. A changed payment state or a start date that passes before the
second approval requires a fresh proposal. Declining leaves all dates unchanged.
Schedule approval itself never charges, refunds or releases money.
