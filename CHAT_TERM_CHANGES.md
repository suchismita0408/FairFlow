# Automatic chat monitoring and agreement proposals

With Gemini configured, every posted text message queues a review of the complete
conversation, speaker roles, current agreement and saved agreement history.
Pending, failed and stale reviews block new approval/signatures, funding and
submissions. Recheck conversation recovers an interrupted or unavailable review.
Unanswered term proposals and disagreements also block; ordinary greetings and
progress messages should not. AI remains fallible and is not a dispute adjudicator.

For mutually agreed changes the monitor automatically drafts exact field values.
Review drafted agreement update previews old/new values. Propose this agreement
update inserts them into the normal counter/amendment process without manual
retyping. Both approvals and signatures are still required. A single milestone
extension on a signed agreement uses a bilateral deadline addendum instead, and
updates the effective agreement only after both accept. Provider coverage must
include delivery plus review/reserve; 29 days is not a universal guarantee.
Protected funded terms, ownership permissions and normal validation still apply.
A proposed update cannot apply after its source agreement changes without a recheck.

AI suggestions are untrusted: field, type and message evidence checks apply;
mutually agreed findings require evidence from both participants. No AI output
signs a contract, resolves a formal dispute or performs a payment. Changes to
funded price/scope that normal amendment protections forbid require an appropriate
separate paid change or settlement; the monitor cannot override that restriction.

Monitoring starts on new messages; old projects are not bulk rewritten or charged.
Existing submitted-work automatic release and reconciliation retain signed rules.

## Deterministic and explicit safeguards

Chat does not edit or sign a contract. Accepted changes must match an approved
agreement or applied bilateral addendum before approval/signing, new checkout,
confirmation/collection of buyer funding, or affected work submission.
These checks run on the backend as well as the interface.

Clear money, price, deadline, relative date-extension, review and scope offers
followed by the other participant's acceptance produce tracked changes. One
message can track both a price and a date change. A quote for additional work
requires a separate matching new milestone rather than replacing the total price.
An offer alone, self-acceptance or rejection is not bilateral acceptance.

Natural language remains ambiguous. **Record term change** beside any chat
message lets participants choose an exact agreement field and value, including
fields beyond the built-in text patterns. Both must confirm that exact record.
This explicit record is the reliable path for unusual wording; the detector
cannot promise to understand every informal sentence or language.

The project displays unresolved accepted changes and links to the agreement or
deadline-extension flow. Editing a draft alone does not permit use of unsigned
terms: approval/signature checks use the sent version, while funding/submission
checks use the active signed agreement plus applied addenda. Existing paid terms
remain protected by the existing amendment rules.

The older price-clarification confirmations cannot bypass this safeguard. If
both sides abandon a chat change, each must explicitly confirm withdrawal using
their account name and a recorded reason. A single withdrawal cannot clear it.

Existing payment review, automatic release, refunds and reconciliation continue
under their previously agreed rules. A new chat offer or agreement cannot be
used as an informal payment pause for previously submitted work. A timely formal
delivery objection remains the mechanism for that pause.
